Grading:

25% on class participation

25% on weekly essays

Weekly Essays:

You will be expected to write a weekly essay each week throughout the semester. During the weeks in which there are lectures and course readings, you will write a question related to the readings or lectures for that week that will be used as a basis for discussion in the discussion section. The kinds of questions and how to prepare them will be discussed in week 1. The questions may not be more that one page in length. They must be in your AI`s hands in electronic form no later than 6 PM Thursday (the day before your discussion section). Late papers will not be accepted.

Grading of the weekly essays will be as follows:

0 no paper or late paper or unacceptable quality

1 question is on topic but shows limited insight or contains errors

2 question is entirely acceptable

3 question shows unusual insight

For the weeks in which student presentations are being made, you will write an analysis of the presentation of any one of the presentations that occurred that week (but not of the presentation of your own group). You should comment on the relevance of the topic to this course, the quality of the research, the quality of the organization, and the quality of the presentation. These evaluations will also be graded on a 0 to 3 scale, and an anonymous copy of your review will be shared with the presenters.

The highest 12 scores on your weekly assignments will be counted toward the weekly assignment portion of your final grade.

Class presentation and final paper:

Your team of 3 to 4 students will make a 25-minute presentation in class and then prepare a final paper on the same topic as your classroom presentation. There is an expectation that your group will have done significant research and analysis, and spent time making agreeable the style of both the presentation and the paper. This should represent the equivalent amount of work for each person on the team as they would do for a major term paper. We will ask each student to identify what they and what the other members of the team contributed to the team effort.
